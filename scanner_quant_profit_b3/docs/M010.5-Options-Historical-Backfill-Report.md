## Executive Summary

M010 was closed with PAPER_ONLY because only **1 trading day** was found in `options_chain_snapshots`. This milestone investigated and found the real situation: **841 trading days** of options data existed in `cotahist_daily` all along. The gap was a **hardcoded `TRADE_DATE = 2026-05-18`** in the collector, not missing data.
